I2024-now

What do we still have to learn from the Treatise on Nomadology after devastating decades of neoliberal austerity and reactionary neofascisms?

This is the question behind the weekly reading group that The Public School in Los Angeles began in 2024, which works through the plateau line by line with an eye toward two things in particular: a philosophical definition of the State, including state thought, and new paths for the war machine.
